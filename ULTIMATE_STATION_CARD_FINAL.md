## 💡 **KEY INNOVATIONS**

### **1. Color-Coded Everything**
- Every element adapts to availability status
- No more guessing - color tells the story
- Works for color-blind users (progress bar + text)

### **2. Information Architecture**
- Status → Name → Location (top)
- Progress bar (instant understanding)
- Details in cards (focused attention)
- Price prominent (call to action)

### **3. Visual Hierarchy**
```
Level 1: Availability badge + Status pill (most important)
Level 2: Progress bar + Station name
Level 3: Detail cards (distance, rating, type)
Level 4: Price badge (action trigger)
```

- Entire card: Tappable
- Chevron: 48px (thumb-friendly)
- Badge: 70px (easy to see)
- All elements: 44px+ minimum

### **5. Accessibility**
- ✅ High contrast text (all white on dark)
- ✅ Text shadows for readability
- ✅ Multiple status indicators (color + text + progress)
- ✅ Large touch targets
- ✅ Clear labels (uppercase, bold)
